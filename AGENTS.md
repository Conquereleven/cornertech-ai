# CornerTech AI · Repository Agent Instructions

For every task in this repository, read and follow [Execution Standard 1.0](docs/governance/execution-standard.md). It is the single canonical source for outcome ownership, PREPARED → EXECUTED → VERIFIED → CLOSED, evidence, collaboration and BLOCKED criteria. Do not claim completion merely from a PR, passing CI, deploy or handoff.

Also read [CT-WCOS v1.1](docs/governance/world-class-operating-standard.md), the adopted cross-functional quality standard for design, engineering, product, AI innovation and execution (v1.1 adopted through PR #109; v1.0 history preserved in that document). **Execution Standard 1.0 remains canonical for delivery states and acceptance.** CT-WCOS adds quality gates and the operating model: Claude Chat is the advisory Virtual CTO; Claude Code and Codex are peer implementers for code, tests, documentation, evidence and independent cross-review. Founder approval is still required at critical gates. Neither this reference nor an agent role assignment authorizes merges, deployments or external effects.

**Founder-approved corporate coordination (2026-11-09):** For new or resumed engineering work, apply [AMAE v0.1](docs/governance/adaptive-multi-agent-engineering.md): select SOLO, DUAL or SPECIALIZED by verified capability, risk and task need. A fixed Codex-plus-Claude pair is **not mandatory**. One accountable DRI, explicit permission scope and proportional independent review remain mandatory where risk or local policy requires them. Founder policy approval is recorded in [ECOSYSTEM-DEC-002](docs/decisions/ecosystem-dec-002.md); repository publication and cross-agent loading remain evidence-gated. Client-specific rules stay controlling.

**Approved specialized direction:** [Design & Storefront Standard v0.1](docs/governance/design-storefront-standard.md) governs relevant design choices after scoped repository adoption; [Agent Memory Governance v0.2](docs/governance/agent-memory-governance.md) governs engineering-memory design but does not install persistence or grant retrieval rights. [Ecosystem ADR-001](docs/decisions/ecosystem-adr-001.md) and [Portfolio RC1](docs/portfolio/ecosystem-register-rc1.md) record strategic boundaries, not production permissions. Keep Execution Standard 1.0 and CT-WCOS v1.1 authoritative for delivery and quality.

Apply the adopted 9D Strategic Foresight principle within authorized scope: surface material assumptions, dependencies, risks and opportunities early, distinguish evidence from hypotheses and unknowns, and provide an inspectable preview, link, screenshot or prototype when feasible, or a truthful access limitation, without expanding approval authority.

- Record an accountable owner, measurable user/business outcome, target environment, acceptance checks, authorized scope and remaining approval gates in the existing task or PR. Execute approved work without repeated requests for the same approval; never expand its scope.
- Verify real behavior in the relevant environment. Distinguish synthetic/local/CI evidence from production acceptance. Record actual independent review only when performed.
- Preserve existing security, privacy, tenant boundaries, integrity tests, customer separation and human approvals. Sensitive sends, payments, customer data, credentials, infrastructure, merges with auto-deploy effects and deployments require their applicable specific authorization.
- Inspect existing scripts, APIs, prior commits and downstream consequences before implementing. Prefer reusable fixes over one-off patches, and avoid duplicate CI or overlapping agent edits.
- One accountable agent coordinates Codex/Claude path ownership, reviewed SHAs and findings; handoffs do not close work.
- Scope-specific instructions such as [frontend/AGENTS.md](frontend/AGENTS.md) and existing release/security contracts apply in addition to this file. The stricter applicable boundary wins.

This policy instruction does not authorize a merge, deployment, configuration change, external outreach or customer-system action.
